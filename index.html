How to Avoid ISP Throttling

Internet service providers sometimes slow down streaming traffic. SubTV IP suggests two simple settings to prevent this.

  1. Use Cloudflare DNS – set your device or router to 1.1.1.1.
  2. Increase buffer / cache to 5,000 ms – this gives your player extra time to load data and reduces stuttering.
